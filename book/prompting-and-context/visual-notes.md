# Chapter 3 visual notes: From a Prompt to a Controlled Agent Run

Status: source-visual plan for the approved Chapter 3 rewrite. No media has been
downloaded, copied, redrawn, generated, or embedded.

## Visual strategy

Chapter 3 will not create new diagrams. The prose, source examples, semantic
lists, and tables must carry the complete teaching argument. A source image is
eligible only when it contributes a relationship that the prose benefits from
seeing and when its exact creator, source, reuse treatment, and accessibility
fallback are recorded.

The current plan has two candidate teaching jobs. Both remain visible
reader-facing placeholders until the user reviews their placement and the
source/reuse path is confirmed.

## C3-V01 — Context engineering then and now

- **Teaching job:** help the reader see the Claude 5 article's central change
  from accumulated rules and upfront detail toward judgment, inspectable
  interfaces, progressive disclosure, and rich references.
- **Medium:** exact source figure from Anthropic's Claude 5 context-engineering
  article; no redraw, crop, relabel, tracing, or stylistic adaptation.
- **Source:** Anthropic, “The new rules of context engineering for Claude 5
  generation models”
  https://claude.com/blog/the-new-rules-of-context-engineering-for-claude-5-generation-models
- **Placement:** after the chapter explains useful context and before it turns
  to meaningful boundaries and verification.
- **Required interpretation:** the caption must say that this is Anthropic's
  guidance for Claude 5 generation models, not a universal history of prompting.
- **Avoid:** implying that examples are generally harmful; implying that every
  product has auto-memory; presenting the source's “then/now” claims as a
  standard.
- **Accessibility and fallback:** the surrounding prose and a compact semantic
  list must express every contrast; alt text should describe the exact rows or
  relationships after the candidate image is selected.
- **Reuse decision still needed:** confirm the exact figure, intrinsic size,
  image URL or owner-supplied file, permission/attribution treatment, and
  whether to store locally or link to the source page.

## C3-V02 — Finding unknowns

- **Teaching job:** make blind spots concrete by separating what the learner
  knows, knows they do not know, and has not yet noticed before a consequential
  implementation begins.
- **Medium:** exact source figure from the Claude Fable field guide; no redraw,
  crop, relabel, tracing, or stylistic adaptation.
- **Source:** Anthropic, “A field guide to Claude Fable: Finding your unknowns”
  https://claude.com/blog/a-field-guide-to-claude-fable-finding-your-unknowns
- **Placement:** inside the optional callout in “Explore before committing,”
  after the text explains interviews, references, and prototypes as discovery
  moves.
- **Required interpretation:** the caption must present Fable as one optional
  product workflow for surfacing unknowns, not a mandatory prompt template.
- **Avoid:** expanding the vocabulary into a new chapter framework; adding a
  custom example; treating brainstorming or prototyping as required steps.
- **Accessibility and fallback:** the callout must name the categories and the
  practical question they prompt; detailed alt text will be written only after
  the exact source figure is selected.
- **Reuse decision still needed:** confirm the exact figure, intrinsic size,
  image URL or owner-supplied file, permission/attribution treatment, and
  whether to store locally or link to the source page.

## Convergence gate

Do not add a third visual unless the user identifies a teaching relationship
that the source examples and prose cannot carry. Do not replace either
placeholder until the exact source figure and provenance treatment are
approved.
