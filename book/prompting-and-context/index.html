Study group guide · Chapter 3 Planned

Prompting and Context Engineering

A planned chapter on communicating the result we need, assembling the information that can change that result, and improving instructions without turning them into a brittle wall of rules.

Planned scope

This chapter will distinguish the immediate prompt from the wider context around a model call. It will explore goals, useful context, output requirements, meaningful boundaries, follow-up steering, conflicting instructions, progressive disclosure, and the tradeoff between helpful guidance and overconstraint.

The final structure is intentionally open. The chapter number may change as the book develops.

Starting resources

  • OpenAI — Prompting A practical foundation organized around goal, context, output, and boundaries, followed by iteration and verification.
  • Anthropic — The new rules of context engineering for Claude 5 generation models A model-generation-specific perspective on simplifying guidance, avoiding conflicting rules, designing interfaces, and using progressive disclosure.
  • Claude public artifact — Complete Context Engineering Guide Optional supplementary material. Treat it as an example collection, not an authoritative specification; individual claims should be checked before they enter the chapter.
