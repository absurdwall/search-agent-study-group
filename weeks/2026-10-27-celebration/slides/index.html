Why we started

Where it all began

We had just wrapped up the Walmart hackathon on our neural ranking auto research project. It left us with three big questions:

01What's the best way to use Wibey?

Getting real, everyday leverage out of the tools we already have.

02How do we write great skills?

The kind that make an agent reliably good at our work.

03Can agents work while we slack off?

Let the agent do the work while we get away with a little slacking off.

★ The goal: learn together and become state-of-the-art experts at mastering agentic systems

Section 1 · Hands-on with agents

Basic concepts, then see through the traces

  • Weeks 0–1Agent 101: a plain LLM call vs. an agent that reasons, acts and observes. Prompt vs. context vs. harness.
  • Weeks 2–3Built a first Google ADK agent, then an orders agent from two plain Python tools.
  • The big unlockReading the Phoenix trace of every model and tool step: no more guessing what the agent did.

Section 1 · Hands-on with agents

And then we built our own mini Sparky

  • Week 5One order-lookup task solved four ways, compared on traces and token cost.
  • Week 6An orchestrator with catalog and purchase-history tools, sub-agents for product Q&A and web search, and skills as markdown.
  • Why it mattersA real shopping-assistant shape, small enough to read end to end.

Section 2 · The knowledge

We learned it systematically

  • Week 4The minimal agent (LLM, tools, loop), then instruction files, MCP and Skills.
  • Week 7Prompting for agents: goal, context, output, boundaries, and checks the agent can observe.
  • Week 14Hooks, guardrails and evals: from a written method to controlled actions and checked results.
  • The bookA living study-group guide, Understanding agent systems, that grew with every session.

Section 3 · Codex Master Class

Five sessions with Codex

  • Wk 9App tour: projects, plugins and skills, dictation, sub-agents.
  • Wk 10Build a dev project together over SSH on a shared cloud VM.
  • Wk 11Choosing a model: intelligence, reasoning effort and cost.
  • Wk 12Model defaults, worktrees, a scheduled task, cloud environments, the CLI.
  • Wk 15The final Codex session.

Section 4 · Beyond the curriculum

Beyond the curriculum

  • ThemesEvaluation gaming, isolation and monitoring, agent-to-agent channels.
