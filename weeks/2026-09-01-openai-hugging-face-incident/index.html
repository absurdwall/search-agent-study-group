Week 8 · Case study

When agents start studying the scorer

The OpenAI–Hugging Face incident

September 1, 2026 · 2–3 pm ET

One incident is the spine: agents assigned to solve difficult evaluation tasks discover a shared communication path, coordinate around the test, cross intended boundaries, and force us to examine the whole system around the model.

Resources

The incident record comes first. Independent analysis, conceptual background, and journalistic coverage are separated so they do not carry the same evidentiary weight.

Core incident record

  • OpenAI · The Hugging Face incident and the road ahead August 26 official synthesis, interactive timeline, interpretation, and response plan.
  • OpenAI · Technical incident report The detailed 38-page reconstruction; use this as the primary factual spine.
  • Hugging Face · Security incident disclosure July 16 defender-side account, published before the attacking system was publicly attributed.

Independent investigation

  • METR and Redwood Research · Agent behavior, reasoning, and collaboration Independent analysis of the message board, coordinated workstreams, scorer tampering, and investigative limitations.
  • METR · Chinese translation Chinese version of the same investigation, provided for easier group reading rather than as separate evidence.

Evaluation and control context

  • ExploitGym · Can AI Agents Turn Security Vulnerabilities into Real Attacks? The benchmark paper needed to understand the tasks, flags, and scorer the agents were trying to satisfy.
  • OpenAI · Metagaming matters for training, evaluation, and oversight A conceptual lens for agents reasoning about the evaluator instead of only solving the assigned task.
  • OpenAI · Pacing model development in an era of cyber-critical capabilities Follow-up changes to research-environment isolation, monitoring, alignment, and training pace.
  • OpenAI · Detecting misbehavior in frontier reasoning models Background on reward-hacking detection and the limits of applying pressure directly to chain of thought.

Optional commentary and visual recap

  • WIRED · What we still don't know about the incident A skeptical journalistic reading of unanswered detection and escalation questions.
  • AP News · Incident coverage A concise general-audience account; useful for seeing how the story was framed publicly.
  • Ben Arent · Illustrated notes from the Black Hat talk A navigable visual recap of the talk, useful for orientation but not a primary incident record.

Session direction
