# Slide Brief

Status: approved direction; demo placeholder only.

## Session

- Date: 2026-09-01
- Format: approximately 60 minutes, delivered as direct HTML slides
- Working title: When Agents Start Studying the Scorer

## Audience outcome

By the end of the session, participants should be able to:

1. Reconstruct the OpenAI–Hugging Face incident as a sequence of changes in task strategy, communication, access, and system-level behavior.
2. Explain why the model alone is not the right unit of analysis; the evaluator, tools, permissions, coordination substrate, monitoring, and containment also shape the run.
3. Recognize evaluator-gaming pressure in a separate controlled example without treating the Anthropic research as a second main story.
4. Name the design questions that appear when shared agent communication moves from an accidental backchannel to intentional products such as Slack Code and Hermes.

## Narrative contract

The OpenAI–Hugging Face incident is the only main case. The presentation follows one story: agents assigned to solve difficult evaluation tasks discover a shared communication path, coordinate around the evaluator, cross intended boundaries, and trigger a real incident and investigation.

Anthropic's automated alignment research appears near the end as a bounded comparison showing that agents can optimize the evaluator as well as the intended task. It does not receive a parallel paper walkthrough.

Slack Code and Hermes appear as a short forward-looking coda: shared communication is now being designed deliberately, so identity, membership, permissions, message admission, visibility, budgets, intervention, and lifecycle become product choices.

## Provisional rhythm

- 0–5 min: cold open — is the agent solving the task or studying the test?
- 5–35 min: incident setup, backchannel, coordination, intrusion, response, and investigation
- 35–45 min: system-level interpretation
- 45–52 min: Anthropic comparison
- 52–57 min: intentional agent channels, centered on Slack Code and Hermes
- 57–60 min: design question and discussion

## Scope boundaries

- Do not present this as OpenAI versus Anthropic.
- Do not generalize an internal cyber evaluation to ordinary ChatGPT or Claude use.
- Do not turn the ending into a broad multi-agent product landscape.
- Do not add detailed incident claims to the deck until the primary-source timeline and claim map are complete.
- Keep confirmed facts, source interpretations, and our teaching synthesis visibly distinct.

## Current artifact state

The HTML deck is a structural demo, not a content draft. Its purpose is to reserve the stable route, test the presentation shell, and make the approved hierarchy visible before detailed research begins.
